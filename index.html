Install on your TV

Open AFTVnews Downloader on your TV and enter 4821603. Select Go, then install the APK when prompted.

You can also download the APK to another device and transfer it to your TV.

  1. Download StreamSeat

    Save the APK on your computer or phone.

  2. Send it to your TV

    Copy the APK to a USB drive or send it with a file-transfer app. Open it with your TV’s file manager.

  3. Open StreamSeat

    If Android asks, allow that file manager to install unknown apps. Install the APK, then open StreamSeat from your apps.
